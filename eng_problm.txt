🔥 1. AI-Based Product Recommendation System

Users waste time searching. Platforms don’t personalize enough.

💡 Solution:

Build a recommendation system that:

Tracks user browsing

Tracks purchases

Suggests similar products

🛠 How to Implement:

Store user behavior in MongoDB

Create recommendation algorithm (basic ML logic)

Show “Recommended for You”

🎓 Why It’s Engineering-Level:

You are solving personalization + data analysis problem.

🔥 2. Smart Inventory Prediction System
🧠 Problem:

Stores lose money due to overstock or out-of-stock products.

💡 Solution:

Predict when stock will run out using sales history.

Example:

If product sells 10 per day
Stock = 50
→ Alert admin: “Restock in 5 days”

🎓 Why It’s Engineering-Level:

You are solving supply chain optimization problem.

🔥 3. Fake Review Detection System
🧠 Problem:

Many e-commerce sites suffer from fake reviews.

💡 Solution:

Build logic to:

Detect too many reviews from same IP

Detect spam keywords

Detect abnormal rating patterns

🎓 Why It’s Engineering-Level:

You are solving trust & fraud detection problem.

🔥 4. Sustainable / Green Shopping Indicator 🌱
🧠 Problem:

Consumers don’t know environmental impact.

💡 Solution:

Add:

Carbon footprint score

Eco-friendly badge

Sustainable product filter

🎓 Why It’s Engineering-Level:

Solves environmental sustainability problem.

🔥 5. Smart Price Optimization System
🧠 Problem:

Pricing is often inefficient.

💡 Solution:

Auto-adjust price based on:

Demand

Stock

Sales velocity

High demand → Increase price slightly

Low demand → Discount automatically

🎓 Why It’s Engineering-Level:

You are solving dynamic pricing problem.
